Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

GradeChimp ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit gradechimp.com (the "Site").

Information We Collect

Data you enter into our tools: When you use our calculators (grades, GPA, study plans), all data you enter is processed entirely in your browser. We do not store, transmit, or have access to any grades, scores, or personal academic information you enter. When you close or refresh the page, your data is gone.

Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we collect standard log data when you visit our site. This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URL. This data is collected through our hosting provider (Vercel) and through Google Analytics/AdSense.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your interests and browsing history. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Analytics cookies: We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve. This data is aggregated and anonymous.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to collect information including your IP address, browser, and browsing activity to provide targeted advertisements. For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, visit Google's Privacy & Terms page.

Children's Privacy

Our tools are designed for students of all ages, including those under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since our calculators process all data locally in the browser and we do not require accounts or logins, no personal academic data is ever transmitted to our servers.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through our site. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Since we don't store your academic data (it stays in your browser), the primary data we protect is standard web server logs.

External Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any site you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@gradechimp.com.
