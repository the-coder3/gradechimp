About GradeChimp

GradeChimp is a collection of free tools built for students. We make grade calculators, GPA tools, and study planners that are fast, clean, and actually useful — no signups, no paywalls, no clutter.

Every tool on this site is 100% free and always will be. We believe students shouldn't have to pay to figure out their grades or plan their study time. That's a basic need, not a premium feature.

Why We Built This

We were tired of grade calculator websites that look like they were built in 2005, covered in pop-up ads, and require three clicks just to enter a number. Students deserve better tools. So we built them.

GradeChimp started as a simple final grade calculator and grew into a full suite of student tools. Every feature was designed based on what students actually need — not what looks good in a pitch deck.

How We Stay Free

GradeChimp is supported by ads. We keep ads minimal and non-intrusive — you'll never see a pop-up or a full-screen takeover. The ads help us keep the lights on so every tool stays free for every student.
